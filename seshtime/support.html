Common questions

Does the timer stay accurate if I lock my screen?

Yes. Sesh Time counts by the clock, so you can lock your phone or switch apps mid-session and the time is right when you come back.

Will the closing bell ring if my phone is locked?

Yes, if you allow notifications. The first time you start a session, Sesh Time asks to send notifications so the closing bell can ring while the app is in the background. If you said no, you can turn them on in Settings › Notifications › Sesh Time. Either way, the session is still timed correctly.

How do I create or edit a preset?

Set up a session on the timer screen, then tap the preset menu above the steppers and choose Save Current Preset as…

To change one later, open Presets from the menu and tap it. You can rename it, change its sets and phases, and pick its sounds.

How do I change the sounds?

Settings › Sounds sets the volume and the default sounds for the start of a session, each new round and the end. Any preset can use its own sounds instead: open it from Presets and choose them under Sounds.

Can I turn off the streak?

Yes. In History, tap the ⋯ button and switch off Show streak. Your history stays exactly as it is.

Does Sesh Time work with Apple Health?

Optionally, yes. Turn on Share with Health in Settings and finished sessions of a minute or longer are saved to Apple Health as mindful minutes. It's entirely opt-in, and Sesh Time never reads anything back from Health.

What data do you collect?

None. There's no account, no analytics and no tracking, and your session history stays on your device. See the privacy policy for the full picture.
